## Settled

- Two different products never share a box (Kathy, 10/26/26).
- Chris scans every box; the paper packing copy is the backup for when
  there's no internet, with a blank per box under its label number, and
  "Type in from paper" lays out the same way (Lucas, 10/26/26).
- A pound product with a box size counts as a known number of boxes (Le Trim
  at 45 lb a box gets numbered blanks) (Lucas, 10/26/26).
- Pallets have tags (P1, P2...) and belong to a shipment: a customer that
  ships direct, or a distributor. Pallet groups are replaced by Ships to in
  Setup. "Pallet 1 of 2" on the manifest counts that shipment's pallets
  (Lucas, 10/26/26).
- "Needs 2 of 3 boxes" counts boxes when the box count is known, pounds when
  a weight is known, otherwise the line stays open until it's marked done
  (Lucas, 10/26/26).
- Nothing is assigned to a customer without a tap. Choosing another customer
  than suggested is logged (Lucas, 10/26/26).
- Le Trim: Kathy types what she actually used (shoulders, half legs, trim),
  so the history shows what's routine; extra trim is labelled Estimated
  until the kill sheet figure is in. It doesn't change the lamb count
  (Lucas, 10/25/26).
- Packing shows the names customers order by (customer products), not
  Mohawk's cut sheet wording.
- Packing lists customers by distributor, then by name.
- Legs are summarised as bone-in, AO and boneless from the customer products
  (BI Legs, AO Legs, BLS Legs).
- Hind shanks stay on the legs: This week shows them as available, not as
  something to plan for, and they never set the lamb count.
- Front shanks never set the lamb count ("we will not kill lambs to get
  shanks").
- Shanks are ordered and counted in packs of 2 (one pack counts as one).
- Le Trim no longer uses up shoulders in the lamb count.
- The order week runs Thursday through Wednesday, named by its packing Monday.
  The app switches to the new week at 12:00 AM Thursday (Kathy, 10/21/26).

  Monday-start week. Kathy agreed to move them forward one week.
- Boxes ship Tuesday or Wednesday.
- No box is planned over 45 lb.
- This week shows front and hind shanks in packs, to match the orders
  (Lucas, 10/21/26).

  customer, counted in the total. The Box Label Report shows "?" for its
  box; the printed label leaves the box type off (Lucas, 10/21/26 and
  10/24/26).

  Print all or selected, starting at any position on a partly used sheet. Each
  box row has a label number for the week that never changes when other
  orders do; prints are logged, and rows changed after printing say "Changed
  since printed" (Lucas, 10/23/26).
