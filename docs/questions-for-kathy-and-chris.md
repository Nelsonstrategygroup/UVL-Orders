## Settled

- Packing shows the names customers order by (customer products), not
  Mohawk's cut sheet wording.
- Packing lists customers by pallet group, then by name.
- Legs are summarised as bone-in, AO and boneless from the customer products
  (BI Legs, AO Legs, BLS Legs).
- Hind shanks stay on the legs: This week shows them as available, not as
  something to plan for, and they never set the lamb count.
- Front shanks never set the lamb count ("we will not kill lambs to get
  shanks").
- Shanks are ordered and counted in packs of 2 (one pack counts as one).
- Le Trim no longer uses up shoulders in the lamb count.
- The order week runs Thursday through Wednesday, named by its packing Monday.
  The app switches to the new week at 12:00 AM Thursday (Kathy, 10/21/26).
- Orders entered before 10/20/26 were filed one week early under the old
  Monday-start week. Kathy agreed to move them forward one week: orders taken
  Thu Oct 8 (and the PCC orders added Wed Oct 14) to Packing Mon Oct 12, and
  orders taken Thu Oct 15 to Packing Mon Oct 19. Nothing goes into the week
  starting Thu Oct 22.
- Boxes ship Tuesday or Wednesday.
- No box is planned over 45 lb.
- This week shows front and hind shanks in packs, to match the orders (need
  110 packs, get 82, short 28 for Mon Oct 19) (Lucas, 10/21/26).
- A product with no box size (Heart, Liver, Kidney) still gets one label per
  customer, counted in the total. The Box Label Report shows "?" for its
  box; the printed label leaves the box type off (Lucas, 10/21/26 and
  10/24/26).
- Box labels print from the app on Avery 5963 sheets (4" x 2", 10 per sheet):
  Print all or selected, starting at any position on a partly used sheet. Each
  box row has a label number for the week that never changes when other
  orders do; prints are logged, and rows changed after printing say "Changed
  since printed" (Lucas, 10/23/26).
- Label layout: the customer and store as one bold name ("PCC #10
  Issaquah"), the quantity in a black box and the product in a green band, a
  red "BOX 2 OF 3" tag only on multi-box orders, "label 14" small in the
  middle, and the box type on the right. Box types stay MVM, Roll Stock,
  Jerky, White, Baby (some paperwork says Mohawk and Rollstock) (Lucas,
  10/23/26 and 10/24/26).
- Every report on Print and download opens as a page to read or print, and
  Box sizes prints the Setup > Boxes grid with a blank Notes column for
  corrections (Lucas, 10/21/26).
- "Last order" is the customer's most recent week with an order before the
  week being looked at. The order screen shows it to read back, with "Last:"
  on each product; the order sheet has a Last order column and lists what
  they usually buy first. On the order sheet, "Invoice batch" replaced
  "Taken by" and "Scheduled delivery" replaced "Packing" (Lucas, 10/22/26).
- A box marked "needs checking" in Setup is simply not used when splitting
  an order (Lucas, 10/21/26).
- Packing is only for packing: Scan boxes, progress, then the customers. The
  Box Label Report, order sheets, the paper packing copy and all the
  spreadsheets are under More > Print and download (Lucas, 10/21/26).
- This week is the weekly overview: the lamb count and any short parts, then
  the week's steps (orders, cut sheet, box labels, packing), then planning.
- Setup is a list of pages, each showing how many items are left to check.
  The "Settings" button is now "My account" (Lucas, 10/21/26).
- Quantity boxes on the order screen take any number of digits again ("80",
  "125", "12.5"); only one digit went in before. For products sold in steps
  (Le Trim, 10 lb), what's typed is kept, with a gentle note: "Le Trim goes
  in 10 lb steps. Did you mean 80?" (Lucas, 10/22/26).
- Requested lines for a later week: "Move to next week" on the order screen,
  or "Request for a later week". They show at the top of Calls and the order
  screen that week ("Requested on Oct 22: 10 BNLS Legs. Confirm, change, or
  drop."), and at the top of that week's order sheet. They count toward the
  lamb count, product totals and the cut sheet, but aren't on Packing or the
  box labels until confirmed. "Same as last week", "Last order" and "What
  they usually buy" use confirmed lines only (Lucas, 10/22/26).
- Undo lasts 30 seconds, with a ✕ to close it, and only shows where there's
  no other way back (Lucas, 10/22/26).
